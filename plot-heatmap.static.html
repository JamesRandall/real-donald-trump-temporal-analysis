Posting rhythm · Truth Social

Year by hour of day

Each cell shows how concentrated posting was in that hour of the day, relative to that year's total output. Read down a column to see one year's daily rhythm; read across a row to see how that hour's share has shifted over time.

Scale mode

Per-year max normalises colour within each column. Every year's darkest cell hits the top of the palette, so each year shows its daily rhythm at full contrast — even a year with very few posts.

Global max uses one shared scale anchored at the largest percentage anywhere in the visible matrix. A year with a sharp peak draws darker than one whose posts are spread evenly — concentration becomes the signal.

Cell values themselves don't change: every cell is already a percentage of that year's posts. Excluding a year via the chips below removes it from the chart and from the global-max calculation.
