Posting rhythm · Truth Social

Month by hour of day

Each cell shows how concentrated posting was in that hour of the day, relative to that month's total output. Defaults to the last two years — widen the date range to see longer arcs.

Scale mode

Per-month max normalises colour within each column. Every month's darkest cell hits the top of the palette, so each month shows its daily rhythm at full contrast — useful when posting volume changed a lot between months.

Global max uses one shared scale anchored at the largest percentage anywhere in the visible range. Months with sharp peaks draw darker than ones whose posts are spread out — concentration becomes the signal.

Cell values themselves don't change: each cell is already a percentage of that month's posts. Changing the date range above re-anchors the global-max calculation.

Drop a CSV (output of analyse-month-hours.ts --normalise) or click "Load sample".
