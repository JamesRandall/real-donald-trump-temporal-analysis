Weekday by hour

Each cell shows the share of posts that fell on a given weekday at a given hour. Saturday and Sunday columns are highlighted — weekend rhythms tend to differ from weekday ones in ways worth looking at. The strip on the right shows each hour's total share, summed across all weekdays.

Scale mode

Across all cells uses one shared scale anchored at the largest percentage anywhere in the matrix. Weekdays with sharp peaks draw darker than ones with even distribution — useful for comparing intensity across the week.

Within each weekday normalises colour per column. Every weekday's darkest cell hits the top of the palette, so each day's daily rhythm reads at full contrast — useful when one day dominates the volume but you still want to see the others' shape.

Cell values themselves don't change: each cell is a percentage of total posts in the filter window.
